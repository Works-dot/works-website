name: Legal translation approval
description: Approved interim English legal links while translations await explicit publication approval.
---

English legal translations require separate user approval before public release, including release in the public GitHub repository. Until approval, English navigation and consent links should point to Hungarian HTML legal pages with an explicit “(Hungarian)” label. Existing English legal URLs may temporarily redirect to the corresponding Hungarian page, without claiming an English translation in hreflang or sitemap.

**Why:** The user approved this interim approach because the previous English-labelled links already led to Hungarian PDFs without disclosing the language mismatch.

**How to apply:** Keep unapproved translations in non-served review files or protected drafts. Treat approval of the interim links as distinct from approval to publish translations.
